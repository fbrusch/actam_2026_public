How to submit.

Categories

  • fidelity: rebuild a groove as close to the original as you can.
  • remix: take a groove and change something on purpose.
  • mashup: combine parts of two or more grooves.

Steps

  1. Create your repository from the template: https://github.com/fbrusch/actam-groove-template (Use this template on GitHub).
  2. Work in solution.ipynb. Use drumkit.py and the Python standard library. The last cell must be play(...) with your result.
  3. Commit and push. On GitHub, open Commits and copy the full commit id (SHA).
  4. One member sends an email from their polimi.it address to francesco.bruschi@polimi.it.

Email

To:      francesco.bruschi@polimi.it
Subject: [ACTAM] lesson04-groove fidelity levee_breaks

repo: https://github.com/your-name/your-repo
commit: 3f2c9e1d8a7b6c5d4e3f2a1b0c9d8e7f6a5b4c3d

members:
teammate.one@mail.polimi.it
teammate.two@mail.polimi.it

public_note:
We could not hear the hi-hat in the intro, so we guessed eighths.

private_note:
Anything you want to tell only the teacher.

Subject

[ACTAM] lesson04-groove <category> <id>. Each groove’s id is on the grooves page. For a mashup, join ids with +: [ACTAM] lesson04-groove mashup levee_breaks+be_my_baby.

Body

  • repo and commit: where your solution is. No GitHub? Leave them out and attach solution.ipynb.
  • members: the polimi.it addresses of the other members, one per line. The sender is a member automatically. Working alone? Leave it out.
  • public_note: optional, shown to anyone who sees your solution.
  • private_note: optional, read only by the teacher.

Resubmitting

You can submit again at any time. The most recent submission counts.
