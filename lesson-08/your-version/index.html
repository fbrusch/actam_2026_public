Your Fra Martino.

Make your own version of Fra Martino in a genre you like: ambient, trap, techno, synth pop, metal, blues, anything. Alone or in a group. There is no deadline.

The rules

  • Keep the tune. The melody is the notes of call, climb, bells and ending, written as degrees. Everything else is yours to change: the scale or the mode, transposition, tempo, rhythm, instruments, the order and the repetitions of the parts.
  • Start from the lesson's tools: Pitch, Clip, Scale and play_clip, from The tools and the notebook from class.
  • Add whatever you need. New functions, new methods, new instruments, new ways of mixing clips: all welcome. Earlier lessons are fair game too, for example the drum kit of lesson 04.

How to send it

One member sends an email from their polimi.it address to francesco.bruschi@polimi.it, with your work in one of two ways:

  • attach the notebook (.ipynb), with any name; or
  • a repository: write its address and the commit id (SHA), as in lesson 04.

The subject only needs to say what it is: Fra Martino or Frère Jacques, and the genre if you like. Everything else in the email is optional.

Email

To:      francesco.bruschi@polimi.it
Subject: Fra Martino trap

repo: https://github.com/your-name/your-repo
commit: 3f2c9e1d8a7b6c5d4e3f2a1b0c9d8e7f6a5b4c3d

members:
teammate.one@mail.polimi.it

public_note:
The bells come in an octave higher in the drop.

private_note:
Anything you want to tell only the teacher.

Optional

  • members: the polimi.it addresses of the other members, one per line. The sender is a member automatically.
  • public_note: a line about your version, shown to anyone who hears it.
  • private_note: read only by the teacher.
  • It helps if the notebook runs from top to bottom and plays the whole track in its last cell, but a few words on how to play it work just as well.

Sending again

You can send a new version at any time. The most recent one counts.
